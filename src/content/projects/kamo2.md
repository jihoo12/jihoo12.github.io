Grows out of Kamo, a small Cartesian cubical kernel, toward a functional surface language that looks ordinary until a program actually works with equality.

- Indexed inductive families with dependent pattern matching
- Path equality (`==`), transport and composition in the surface syntax
- A first higher inductive type milestone around `Circle`
