Every shadow, highlight and rim light on the page comes from a single light. It can follow the cursor, or be the real sun at the visitor's location and time of day.

- About 5 kB gzipped, zero dependencies
- Real projection instead of guessed offsets
- Respects `prefers-reduced-motion`
